# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise code, not
people.

## The standard

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Examples of behaviour that make this a good place to work: giving feedback on
the substance of a change, accepting feedback on your own, admitting when you
were wrong, and explaining your reasoning so someone else can disagree with it
usefully.

Examples of behaviour that do not: personal attacks, harassment of any kind,
publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to the maintainer at the address on the GitHub profile.
Reports are handled privately. Maintainers who do not follow this in good faith
may face consequences determined by the project's other maintainers.

Consequences escalate in proportion: a private correction, a public correction,
a temporary ban, a permanent ban.
